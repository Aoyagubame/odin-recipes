Ice Cream

A delicious Ice cream, yum!

Ingriedients

  • Cow
  • Love
  • Time
  • Cold Weather

Instructions

  1. Talk to cow, softly.
  2. Let them know you are making ice cream and they should help.
  3. Wait for cold day, plan a date.
  4. On cold day, (good job waiting) take cow to mountain top.
  5. Add mountain to ingriedeients above.
  6. Milk cow in to snow.
  7. Scoop in to bowl.
  8. Tell the cow you are very proud.
  9. Viola (flourish)
  10. You are done.
